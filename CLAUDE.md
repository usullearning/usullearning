# CLAUDE.md — Usul Learning

Usul Learning distributes a **free public digital review edition**. Readers get the book/content free; there is an option to voluntarily support the work. Keeping that distinction clean is the central rule of this repo. The UCS-wide rules (CLAUDE-GLOBAL-UCS.md) apply in full.

## Support/access separation — hard rules

- **Book/content access must never depend on support.** No gating, no "support to unlock," no nagging that implies access is conditional, no dark patterns steering users into paying before accessing.
- **Support is voluntary only.** All support wording must make voluntariness unmistakable.
- **Never present support as:**
  - a **payment** for the book or any service (it buys nothing; the content is free),
  - **zakat** or any religiously obligated giving,
  - a **tax-benefit donation** (no 80G or deduction implications — none exist),
  - **CSR** or corporate-giving framing,
  - a **required or expected contribution** ("suggested price," "pay what it's worth" pressure framing).
- Wording like "if this benefited you and you wish to support the work, you may" is the right register. Anything stronger needs founder review.

## Legal and payment wording

- All UPI/payment/legal wording (support page text, payee identity, receipts, terms, privacy) is `[REQUIRES LAWYER/CA REVIEW — DO NOT PUBLISH AS-IS]`.
- **No misleading charity, FCRA, Udyam, or tax claims.** Do not imply charitable registration, foreign-contribution eligibility, MSME status, or tax benefits of any kind. Do not use "donation" language that implies charitable status.

## Sharia sensitivity

- Support wording sits directly on Sharia-sensitive ground (charity framing, honesty about what payment does, no religious-obligation implication). Any change to support/payment wording gets `[SHARIA-SENSITIVE — founder/scholar review required]`.
- Never call support "sadaqah," "zakat," or attach religious merit claims to it.

## General

- No inflated claims about the book, readership, or impact. No invented reader counts or testimonials.
- Keep public content free of internal UCS material (College Programme, associate model, tracker, internal workflows).
- Small, surgical changes; founder approves and commits; never push.
- After work, report per the UCS standard (files changed, unchanged, validation, risks, flags, founder decisions, commit command not executed).
