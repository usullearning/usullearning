Our Approach

Grounded in Tradition

Each resource follows a structured framework with references from the Islamic tradition preserved over centuries.

Qur'an & Sunnah

Teachings are presented with reference to the Qur'an, hadith literature, and the primary sources as understood by the scholarly tradition.

Shafi'i Madhab

Legal rulings follow the established methodology of Imam al-Shafi'i and the generations of scholars who preserved and developed this school.

Scholarly Integrity

The aim is to present established knowledge clearly, without deriving new rulings or introducing novel positions.
