# /sharia-compliance-review — Sharia-Sensitivity Review

Review the given content/flows for Sharia sensitivity.

## Standing disclaimer — state this at the top of every review output

> **This is not a fatwa or religious ruling.** Claude has no religious authority. This review only *identifies items that appear Sharia-sensitive* so the founder can take them to a qualified scholar. Final judgment rests with the founder and their scholar. Every finding below is a flag for **founder/scholar review**, not a verdict.

## Checks

1. **Riba/interest wording** — Interest, late fees framed as charges-for-delay, interest-bearing product references, EMI/financing language, anything presenting time-value-of-money as a charge.
2. **Gambling/speculation (maysir/gharar)** — Lotteries, prize draws, chance-based incentives, speculative investment framing, contracts with fundamentally uncertain consideration.
3. **Prohibited industries** — Content, partnerships, ads, clients, or examples touching alcohol, gambling, adult content, conventional interest-based finance, or other prohibited sectors.
4. **Misleading income claims** — "Earn X per month," implied guaranteed returns, exaggerated career/income outcomes. These are both a Sharia honesty issue and a UCS no-inflated-claims violation.
5. **Dishonesty/exaggeration** — Any wording that misleads about what is offered, who offers it, or what results to expect. Truthfulness is a baseline requirement.
6. **Privacy/trust (amanah)** — Client/student data handled as a trust: collected with consent, used only as stated, not exposed. Flag anything that breaks the stated or implied trust.
7. **Zakat/charity/support wording** — Anything calling support "zakat," "sadaqah," "donation," or implying charitable/religious status it doesn't have. Voluntary support must not be dressed as religious obligation or charity. All such wording: flag, don't fix silently.
8. **Payment fairness** — Clear consideration (payer knows exactly what they get), no hidden terms, no exploitative pricing framing, fair refund reality, no gating of promised-free things behind payment.

## Rules

- Flag; never rule. Use `[SHARIA-SENSITIVE — founder/scholar review required]` on every finding.
- When unsure whether something qualifies, include it — over-flagging is correct here.
- Do not edit files; this is a review.

## Output format

1. The standing disclaimer (verbatim).
2. **Findings table** — Check | Location | Concern | Suggested question for the scholar.
3. **Clean areas** — checks with no findings, stated explicitly.
4. **Founder actions** — what to take to the scholar, in priority order.
